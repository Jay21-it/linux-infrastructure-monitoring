Before committing:
- Blur or remove internal IPs and hostnames.
- Remove tokens, usernames, email addresses, secrets, and customer data.
- Confirm the evidence contains no employer-confidential material.
- Update `docs/VALIDATION.md` to reflect what the evidence actually proves.
